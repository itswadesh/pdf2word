What this converter does not do

  • No account, no email address and no sign-up.
  • No advertising, and no tracking that follows you to other websites.
  • Your uploaded files and their contents are never sent to Google or to anyone else, and no analytics event ever carries a file name or its text.
  • A cookie that only lets this browser find the files it uploaded, and (where analytics runs, see above) the cookies Google Analytics sets. If you pick a language for scanned pages, your browser remembers that choice too.
  • Nothing is added to the Word file: no watermark and no link back.
