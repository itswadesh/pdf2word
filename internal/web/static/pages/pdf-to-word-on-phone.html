On iPhone and Android

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari and tap Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick the PDF from Files, or from iCloud Drive.
  3. Press Convert to Word. The DOCX is saved to Downloads in the Files app.
  4. Open it with Microsoft Word or Pages to edit it.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome and tap Choose a PDF.
  2. Pick the PDF from Files, Drive or Downloads.
  3. Press Convert to Word. The DOCX is saved to the Downloads folder.
  4. Open it with Microsoft Word or Google Docs to edit it.

Good to know

The file goes over your connection, so a large PDF on mobile data takes a while to upload before the first page shows. On Wi-Fi it is quicker.

You can watch each page being read, as on a computer, and the Word file is the same: the fonts, tables, pictures and pages of the original.

Questions

Can I convert PDF to Word on my phone?

Yes. Open this page in your phone’s browser, choose the PDF from your files, and the Word document is saved to your downloads.

What file do I get?

A .docx file, the format Word has used since 2007. Google Docs, LibreOffice Writer and Apple Pages open it too.

Is there a limit on file size or number of files?

Up to 100 MB per file, and as many files as you like, one after another.
