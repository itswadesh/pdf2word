DOC vs DOCX: what is the difference?

Both are Word documents. DOC is the older format Word used until 2007; DOCX is the one it has used since. Here is how they differ, and which to use.

DOC and DOCX side by side

DOC and DOCX compared
QuestionDOCDOCX
Used by WordWord 97 to Word 2003Word 2007 and later
Inside the fileA binary formatA zip of XML files
StandardPublished by Microsoft, not an open standardOffice Open XML, ISO/IEC 29500
SizeLargerUsually smaller, because it is compressed
MacrosCan carry themCannot; a Word file with macros is DOCM
Opens inWord, and most other word processorsWord, Google Docs, LibreOffice Writer and Apple Pages

Which should you use?

DOCX, unless someone needs to open the file in Word 2003 or older. It is smaller, it is an open standard, and a DOCX cannot carry macros, which makes it safer to open from someone else.

To make a DOC from a DOCX, open it in Word and choose File, Save As, Word 97-2003 Document. Some newer formatting may be simplified.
