PDF to Word Converter

Just drop any PDF, and it will convert to editable Word format. It works for scanned images as well.

Text PDFs keep their fonts, headings, tables and pictures. Scanned pages are read with text recognition, so the result is editable text rather than a picture of the page.

Files stay on the computer running the converter and are deleted an hour after conversion. The Word file downloads by itself when it is ready.
